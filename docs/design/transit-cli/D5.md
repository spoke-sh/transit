# D5 — Preserve drafts when canceling history search

[Review index](README.md) · [Previous: D4](D4.md)

Status: **Accepted and implemented**. Terminal checks and their limits are
recorded in the [proof ledger](../../../tests/README.md#cancel-interactions-without-clearing-drafts--2026-10-24). The
[terminal guide](../../../apps/transit-cli/docs/console.md#terminal-editing-and-history)
owns current behavior.

## Question

What should Esc restore when a history filter starts from existing draft text,
and how should that differ from typing a filter after entering an empty search?

## Proposal

Keep the saved draft and the search filter separate. Ctrl-R captures the whole
draft and cursor, copies its text into the filter, and previews the newest match.
Further typing edits only the filter; repeated Ctrl-R finds older matches.

Esc or Ctrl-G cancels search and restores the captured draft and cursor. Starting
from an empty draft therefore returns to an empty draft, even if a filter was
typed later. Tab or Enter accepts the selected history statement as draft text;
undo restores the preceding draft. Acceptance never submits a statement.

Use the same distinction throughout the editor: Esc dismisses completion and
leaves ordinary draft text intact. Ctrl-C clears the draft, including the saved
original while searching. Ctrl-U clears only the filter during search.

## Review outcome

Accepted: seed search from the full draft; keep filter edits separate; Esc always
cancels or dismisses, and Ctrl-C clears. The alternative of changing Esc only
inside history search was not selected. This supersedes [D3](D3.md)'s empty
initial filter and clear-on-Esc behavior. Other history and completion controls
retain their existing meanings.
