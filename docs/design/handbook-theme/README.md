# Handbook theme

[Design index](../README.md)

Carry the visual grammar of the selected [Transit artwork](../transit-artwork/D1.md#direction-and-palette-comparison)
into the Docusaurus homepage and reading experience.

Current review: [D6 — Through the surface](D6.md). Status: **Accepted**.
Dark mode becomes a radiographic view into depth. Scrolling down moves the camera
in along z; scrolling up pulls it back through the same layers. D6 includes new
artwork, the original interactive study and a reversible-motion recording.
The reviewer merged the study and requested implementation. The homepage now
uses its dark composition and depth passage, with static narrow/reduced-motion
views; the existing chapter copy and accepted light scenes remain intact.
See [Figure 7 — integrated opening](D6.md#figure-7--integrated-handbook-opening),
[Figure 8 — integrated passage](D6.md#figure-8--integrated-depth-passage) and the
[production motion recording](previews/d6-implemented-depth.webm).

The accepted light-mode baseline remains [D5 — Fabric and hidden pockets](D5.md).
After the resonance zones, the fabric straightens, develops pockets and ends in
an oblique opening with an unresolved interior. Native scrolling reveals the
continuous drawing. D5 Figures 3 and 4 are selected; the final oblique pocket
briefly remains in view as the reader passes it on a tall desktop screen.
D4 Figures 3 and 4, the graphite Internals and Architecture diagrams, are also
accepted and remain unchanged.

**D3 Figure 4 — R1 — Activity** is selected and implemented with the exact lateral
basin artwork from D3 Figure 1. The homepage keeps D1 Figure 1's opening composition
and the selected Activity copy. The other resonance alternatives remain available
for comparison.
