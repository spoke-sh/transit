## Exit evidence

- Public searchable reference, primary sources, exact measurement boundaries and
  intended-surface mapping, with current and proposed behavior distinguished.
- Runnable Zig public-package consumer, independent SELECT/Arrow and mutation
  byte oracles, explicit null/empty/page boundaries, and finite budgets.
- Complete machine inventory, exact blocker probes, duplicate/missing-result
  rejection, failure reports and a nonzero full-coverage gate.
- Raw measured samples and revision/build/fixture/environment provenance; no
  synthetic TPC scores or unpaired speedup claims.
- Focused SQL and harness proofs, full `just check`, committed-HEAD `just ci-clean`
  evidence and a draft PR; failures and unexecuted lanes stay explicit.

## Deferred full-suite execution

Full benchmark execution needs standard generators/scales and query variants,
complete schemas and data validation, result/transaction oracles, load/refresh
and concurrency schedules, correct public admission/commit/read paths and disk
recovery. TPC requirements remain additional to Transit's own authorization and
uncertain-outcome semantics. Inventory completion alone satisfies none of these
execution gates. Further implementation gets its own roadmap scope after the
prerequisite contracts are available; issue #35 retains that outstanding work.
