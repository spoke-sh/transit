# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

Status: Proposed  
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What concrete problem and constraints require a decision? Link the relevant
scope, architecture, contracts, and evidence.

## Decision

State the choice and its boundaries. Identify what remains undecided.

## Alternatives

Compare viable alternatives against the actual requirements. Explain why the
selected option fits better and what would justify reconsidering it.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, operational implications, failure behavior, migration
needs, and reversibility.

## Validation

List the experiment, test, or measurement that supports the decision. Separate
observed results from assumptions and work still required.
